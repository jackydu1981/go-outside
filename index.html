One-time download

Your missions are written by Gemma, an open-weight model from Google, running entirely on this device. It needs a one-time download of about 1.5 GB. After that it works offline, and nothing you do here leaves your phone.

On mobile data? Pick "Lighter model" on the How it works page, or use a hand-written mission for now.

    How it works
    • Gemma writes the mission on your device. It runs in your browser on your GPU through WebLLM and WebGPU. No AI server, no account, no API key.
    • Weather from Open-Meteo. If you allow location, your position is rounded to about 10 km before the weather request. It is never saved.
    • Your journal stays here. Notes live in this browser's local storage and nowhere else.
    • Works offline after the first load: the app and the model are cached.
